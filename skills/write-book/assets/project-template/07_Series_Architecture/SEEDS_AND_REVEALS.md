# Seeds, reveals and knowledge

## Chain of setup and payoff

| ID | Truth of the world / motif | First seed: plan and written scene | Later development | Planned payoff | Written payoff | Dependencies | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Statuses: proposal / accepted plan / seeded / developed / paid off / deliberately open / abandoned. When abandoning, record how the traces already written in the prose were handled.

## Knowledge at boundary points

| End of unit / volume | Fact | Who knows and how | Who suspects or misunderstands | What the reader knows | What remains secret | Scene of transfer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## False leads

| Lead | Real cause of the appearance | Who believes it and why | Earlier signals of its limits | Fair reinterpretation | Scenes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

A reveal plan does not pass knowledge to characters. A flashback can inform the reader without changing what the main characters know. Check every essential payoff backwards and every seed forwards.
