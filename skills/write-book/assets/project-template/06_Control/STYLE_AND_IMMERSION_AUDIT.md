## Check after changes

- Are the narrator's voice and the characters' distinctness preserved?
- Was only excess removed, not the cause of a decision?
- Do transitions, props and knowledge stay consistent?
- Has the new version become a set of short slogans or random gestures?
- Which places were reread and what remains unchecked?

Do not give an "AI percentage" or a numerical guarantee of immersion. Phrases from the scanner are candidates to assess, not automatic errors.
