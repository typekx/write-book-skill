# Light mode

For short stories, short forms, standalone books without a series, samples and quick editing jobs. The goal is the same care for the text with fewer documents. The safeguards in `SKILL.md` apply unchanged.

## What stays

- A profile in a few sentences: genre, audience, narration, tone, length, content boundaries.
- A short style profile if the author gave passages; otherwise labelled assumptions.
- One list of facts and characters (names, ages, relationships, forms of address, important objects), updated after every unit.
- A comparison with the previous version for every correction.
- Reading the whole after changes.

## What goes

Series architecture, volume cards, the seed matrix, evidence and violence registers, the module tracker, the part correction loop, the handoff package. The project template is not needed.

## Short card

Before a unit, enough is: POV, place and time, the character's goal, obstacle, change at the end, last sentence or hook.

## Three-point report

1. **What was produced:** file, word count, scenes in one sentence each.
2. **Facts and changes:** new facts, departures from the plan, questions for the author (or "none").
3. **Check:** what was checked (reading, length, version comparison, tool), what was not, limitations.

## When to switch to full mode

- Another volume appears, or the text grows beyond about 20,000 words with many threads.
- The work is to move to another model or application.
- The fact list starts to drift from the prose, or several versions of the manuscript appear.

When switching, move the fact list into character cards and the timeline, and keep the three-point reports as history.
