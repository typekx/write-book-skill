# Immersion analysis

Immersion means being able to follow and live through a scene within its convention. It does not mean constant action, the maximum number of details or the absence of reflection.

## Two reading passes

First read as a reader: where are orientation, tension, trust or emotional contact lost? Record the moment and the reaction. Then establish the mechanism and the correction in the [audit card](../assets/project-template/06_Control/STYLE_AND_IMMERSION_AUDIT.md). An aesthetic impression is not in itself a canon error.

| Area | Question | Disruption |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Where, when and through whose perception are we? | Unmarked transition, unjustified change of point of view. |
| Causality | Why does this action happen now? | Missing cause of a decision, a convenient coincidence. |
| Agency | Who chooses and pays the price? | The protagonist only receives information; the world arranges itself to their needs. |
| Space and body | Is the action physically possible? | Teleportation, a forgotten wound, a conversation that could not be overheard. |
| Emotion | What changes and where does it come from? | The narrator announces a breakthrough the scene does not justify. |
| Knowledge | Is the mystery fair to the point of view? | Withholding the POV character's natural thought only for an artificial puzzle. |
| Exposition | Is the information needed and available now? | Experts explain the obvious to each other for the reader. |
| Concreteness | Does the detail come from the character's attention and work in the scene? | A catalogue of smells and textures unrelated to the action. |
| Subtext | Do the speakers have their own goals and evasions? | Everyone immediately names their motives and states full theses. |
| Rhythm | Does narrative time match the importance of the moment? | A drawn-out trifle, a summary skipping a key choice. |
| Consequences | What remains of the experience in later scenes? | Reset of conflict, grief, fear or trust. |
| Reveal | Can the reader connect the premises themselves? | A gesture, its explanation and a summarising punchline convey the same thing. |

## Order of corrections

First fix contradictions or impossible facts, then causality, orientation and access to information. Only then exposition, rhythm and images. Swapping words does not remove a lack of motivation.

A needed thought or detail can restore continuity; cutting alone is not always an improvement. In a quiet scene, tension can come from evasion, refusal, a change in closeness or an open question. Do not add danger for compulsory intensity.

After editing, read the scene with its entry and its consequence. Check that no secret has been revealed too early, no premise of a decision removed and the narrator not changed. At module level check serial openings, conversations at the table, farewells and final gestures.

Do not rate immersion as a percentage. Give the places, causes, effect and result of rereading.
